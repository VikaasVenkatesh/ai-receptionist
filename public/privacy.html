Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

This policy explains how the Spear Flow AI Receptionist ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you call a business that uses our AI phone receptionist, including Han Kim, DC. The receptionist answers calls, answers questions about the business, and books appointments on the business's behalf.

Information we collect

  • Phone number you call from.
  • Call content: your speech is converted to text so the receptionist can understand and respond. We keep a written transcript of the conversation.
  • Details you provide: your name, email address, preferred appointment date and time, and reason for the visit.
  • Call details: time and duration of the call.

How we use your information

  • To answer your questions and book, confirm, or change your appointment.
  • To send appointment confirmations and reminders by email, and by text message where you have given consent.
  • To keep a record of your call and appointment for the business you contacted.
  • To maintain and improve the receptionist's quality and reliability.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Text messages

By booking an appointment by phone, you agree to receive appointment-related messages, such as confirmations and reminders, at the number you called from. Message frequency varies; message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Mobile numbers and text-messaging consent are never shared with or sold to third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

Service providers

We rely on trusted providers to operate the receptionist. They process information only to provide their service to us:

  • Twilio: phone calls and messaging
  • Deepgram: speech-to-text and voice generation
  • Anthropic: AI language processing
  • Google: calendar scheduling and email delivery
  • Railway: application hosting
  • The business's customer relationship management (CRM) system, where call records are stored

We may also disclose information if required by law.

Data retention

Call transcripts and appointment records are kept only as long as needed to provide the service to the business you contacted, or as required by law. Live call audio is processed in real time and is not stored as a recording.

Security

Information is transmitted over encrypted connections and access is limited to those who need it to operate the service. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete your information, or opt out of messages, by contacting us at the address below. To opt out of text messages at any time, reply STOP. Every email includes the business's contact details.

Children

The service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
